<objective>
Make the single shared `.dgt` writer deterministic so byte-identity becomes a real,
testable contract. This is the HARD PREREQUISITE for DAT-01 (tracked blocker
`.planning/todos/pending/dat-parity-gate-is-a-skip.md`): the writer today lets the
platform C library decide the sixth decimal (10 lines differ by exactly 1e-6 between
the two native builds) and emits the platform line terminator (CRLF on Windows, LF on
macOS on every line). Adopt todo option (a) "make it byte-true" (round in R + pin the
terminator) over option (b) tolerance — per RESEARCH Assumption A2, recommended default.

Purpose: DAT-01 compares ONE writer against itself; that only holds if the writer is
deterministic. No second serializer is introduced (RESEARCH Anti-Patterns).
Output: a rounded, EOL-pinned writer in `3dDigitize.main.r` and a determinism test.

PLANNER DECISION (A2, recommended default, user may override): pin the terminator to
CRLF (`\r\n`), matching the in-repo `mergeDgt` precedent (3dDigitize.main.r:3440) and
GUImorph's Windows oracle corpus (`windows-authored-roundtrip.dgt`). Consequence,
stated as a limitation: re-saving the LF `mac-authored-roundtrip.dgt` will emit CRLF
and will NOT byte-round-trip — that is the documented external `-rewrite` finding, not
a regression (see 05-06 and the todo).
</objective>
