<objective>
Add the browser→R pick round-trip to the existing mixed httpuv app: a token-guarded
POST route `/<token>/pick` that stores each hit coordinate in a server-owned landmark
array, plus the R-side accessor that reads it back (server-owns-state). This delivers the
R half of PICK-01 and re-asserts CMP-01 (the native `tkogl2` oracle stays loadable and
`transport.R` never touches its load path).

Purpose: R must own the authoritative landmark array; the browser only reports clicks.
The route reuses the Phase 3 `/close` precedent (a second `excludeStaticPath` subpath on
the same server), preserving loopback-only bind, the ≥128-bit token guard, and the
no-path-to-filesystem-join invariant (T-2-02).

Output: extended `R/transport.R` (registry `.gmw_picks`, handler `.gmw_pick_handler`,
mixed-app wiring, R accessors) and a new `test-picking-transport.R`.
</objective>
